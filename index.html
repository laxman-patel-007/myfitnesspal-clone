Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MyFitnessPal.

Is MyFitnessPal free to use?

Yes, MyFitnessPal offers an extensive free version with complete food logging, exercise tracking, weight progress, and access to the 14+ million food database. We also offer a Premium subscription with advanced nutrient analytics and custom macro breakdowns.

How does MyFitnessPal calculate my calorie and macro goals?

We use the validated Mifflin-St Jeor equation to compute your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) from your age, height, weight, and biological sex, adjusted by your daily activity level and desired weekly pace of change.

What nutrition data can I track besides calories?

You can track carbohydrates, protein, fat, fiber, sodium, sugar, water intake, and exercise energy expenditure across every meal of the day.

Can I test this project immediately as a portfolio demo?

Yes! Click the 1-Click Demo Drive button in the top navigation or hero banner to instantly experience an active account with 14 days of history, meal logs, and interactive charts.
